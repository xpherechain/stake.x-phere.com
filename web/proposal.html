XP Union Vault is a non-custodial staking product on Xphere, funded by the foundation's Union validator revenue. What the product does

Each day the foundation's validator revenue flows into the vault and is split:

60% → stakers  streamed per second
40%+ → burned  forever

The staker pool is priced against the full stake cap, so each staker earns their own share regardless of how full the vault is — APR stays roughly constant as TVL grows, and the yield belonging to unfilled capacity is burned instead. Rewards come from real node revenue, never from new token emissions.

Why partners take this

Nothing to build

Integration is a URL: ?ref=yourslug. No contract calls, no key custody, no backend. You can be live in a day.

Trustless accounting

Your originated TVL lives in partnerTVL() and indexed events. You verify our numbers, not the other way round.

Your users lose nothing

Referred users earn exactly what direct stakers earn. The revenue share is paid by the foundation, never skimmed from users.

A product users understand

Real yield plus a visible daily burn — a deflationary story that is easy to explain and easy to verify.

How it works, end to end

STEP 1

Agree terms

We register your partnerId on-chain (one transaction). Revenue-share terms are agreed individually.

STEP 2

Share the link

Point users to stake.x-phere.com/?ref=you. Attribution is captured on their first deposit and is permanent.

STEP 3

Verify & settle

Watch your TVL accrue on-chain. The foundation settles your revenue share against that public record.

Commercial terms

Revenue share is calculated from the TVL you originate, using the on-chain partnerTVL record as the single source of truth, and is paid by the foundation separately — it is never deducted from your users' rewards. Rates, settlement cadence and payout wallet are agreed per partner; we are happy to discuss a structure that fits how you work. Optional per-partner capacity limits can also be set on-chain.

What we can and cannot promise

  • Verifiable: attribution, TVL, burns, solvency and governance are all public on-chain and independently checkable — including by you.
  • Non-custodial: user principal is never deployed, re-delegated or lent. It is returned in full after the 7-day cooldown, even if rewards stop.
  • Immutable logic: no proxies, no upgradeability. Operational parameters move only through a 48-hour on-chain timelock.
  • Variable, not guaranteed: APR follows actual node revenue. We do not promise a rate, and we would rather set expectations conservatively than oversell.
  • Early stage: the vault launched in a guarded phase with a reduced cap, raised in steps as it proves itself. Early partners grow with it.
