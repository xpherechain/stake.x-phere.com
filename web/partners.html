How partnering works

STEP 1

Get your slug

The Foundation registers your partnerId on-chain (one transaction). Terms are arranged individually.

STEP 2

Share one link

Send users to stake.x-phere.com/?ref=yourslug. Attribution is captured automatically — their yield is identical to staking directly.

STEP 3

Verify & settle

Your routed TVL accrues to partnerTVL(id) on-chain. Revenue share is settled by the Foundation against that public record.

Verify it yourself — trust the chain, not our dashboard

# your attributed TVL (bytes32 id = keccak256 of your slug)
PID=$(cast keccak "yourslug")
cast call 0x… \
  "partnerTVL(bytes32)(uint256)" $PID --rpc-url …
Every Deposited event also carries the indexed partnerId, so you can reconstruct your full TVL history independently.
